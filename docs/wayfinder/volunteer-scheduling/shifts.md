## Question

Which rules make a shift assignment valid, and how do organizers and volunteers create, change, and withdraw assignments?

Preserve the confirmed workflow:

- A shift specifies a stand, start/end time, and required number of workers within a particular edition's stand participation.
- Organizers assign workers directly or approve volunteer shift requests, including requests submitted without a login through valid registration links.
- Every volunteer shift request requires organizer approval. Pending requests do not reserve places; hard capacity is enforced on approval or direct assignment.
- Existing members and valid registration-link holders may request shifts at participating stands in the Current Edition. Approved underway/future assignments establish lasting membership; correcting past shifts leaves membership unchanged.
- The feature has no automatic schedule generation.

Resolve time boundaries and timezone, overlapping shifts across stands, capacity and concurrent signup, availability and any required eligibility constraints, organizer overrides, withdrawal, shift changes/deletion, and what happens to existing assignments after membership is revoked.

Keep scheduling validation separate from authorization. Describe concrete acceptance scenarios and the minimum information the UI must explain when a requested assignment is invalid.
