# Herbstfest Organization

Shared language for organizing the Herbstfest.

## Language

**Organizing team**:
The group responsible for organizing the Herbstfest.

**Volunteer worker**:
A person who helps staff the Herbstfest on a voluntary basis.

**Worker record**:
The shared record for a volunteer worker across stands and festival editions. It may exist without a linked login.

**Worker directory**:
The shared collection of worker records.

**Stand registration link**:
A shareable registration link for a stand's schedule in a particular festival edition.

**Shift registration link**:
A shareable registration link targeting a particular shift.

**Account connection link**:
A worker-specific link that lets its recipient connect a login to an existing worker record.

**Festival edition**:
A particular occurrence of the Herbstfest, identified by a year label, with a start day and an end day.
_Avoid_: Year, when referring to the event itself rather than its label.

**Current Edition**:
The festival edition designated as current for the Herbstfest.

**Selected edition**:
The festival edition a global organizer is currently working with. It can differ from the Current Edition.

**Stand**:
A distinct area of festival work with its own organizing team and volunteer schedule.
_Avoid_: Work area, when referring to this same concept.

**Stand participation**:
The inclusion of a stand in a particular festival edition.

**Stand organizer**:
A member of the team responsible for a stand in a particular festival edition.

**Global organizer**:
A member of the organizing team responsible for coordinating the Herbstfest as a whole.

**Stand membership**:
A continuing association between a volunteer worker and a stand that persists across editions and after assignments end or are cancelled.
It can be established by an organizer adding the worker or confirming an assignment to an underway or future shift; corrections to past shifts leave it unchanged.
_Avoid_: Shift assignment, when referring to this continuing association.

**Shift**:
A time interval for volunteer work at a particular stand, with a capacity defined by its stand organizers.

**Shift capacity**:
The maximum number of volunteer workers who may be assigned to a shift.

**Shift request**:
A volunteer's request for a place in a shift, pending an organizer's approval.

**Shift assignment**:
A volunteer worker's confirmed placement in a particular shift.
_Avoid_: Stand membership, when referring to a particular placement.

**Schedule**:
The collection of shifts and worker assignments for a stand in a particular festival edition.

**Organizer invitation**:
A private invitation to receive a specified global-organizer or edition-specific stand-organizer role.

**Disabled participation**:
A stand’s retained but inactive participation in a festival edition, preserving its schedule and history.
